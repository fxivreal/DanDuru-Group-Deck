================================================================================
         DANDURU GROUP — 3-MINUTE FACTORY TOUR VIDEO SCRIPT
         (DanDuru Industries + FlourishScent)
         Record on your phone (landscape, good lighting)
================================================================================

PURPOSE: Build trust. Let investors SEE that the equipment exists, the factory
is real, and you are ready to scale. This video should feel authentic — not
over-produced. Speak naturally.

TOTAL LENGTH: 2 minutes 45 seconds — 3 minutes max

TONE: Confident, honest, professional. No hype. Just show what exists and
explain what the investment will unlock.

================================================================================
SCENE 1: FACTORY EXTERIOR / ENTRANCE (15 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Stand outside the factory gate or entrance. Show the building and
the DanDuru / DanFam signage if available. Natural light.

SCRIPT:
"I'm Chikwendu Ifeanyi Felix, Group Managing Director of DanDuru Industries —
the parent company of DanDuru Group. This is our production facility in Ogba,
Lagos — where we package sugar, chilli pepper, and other food products for the
Nigerian mass market, and where our subsidiary FlourishScent produces perfume
oils and sprays."

================================================================================
SCENE 2: VFFS PACKAGING MACHINE (40 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Walk to the main packaging machine. Show the VFFS machine — the
vertical form-fill-seal unit. If it's not running (due to skeletal ops),
stand next to it. Point to key parts: the film roll area, the filling
mechanism, the sealing jaws, the output conveyor.

SCRIPT:
"This is our primary packaging machine — a VFFS unit that automatically
forms, fills, and seals sachets. It handles both granulated sugar and
chilli pepper. This machine can produce over 150,000 sachets per shift.
It was supplied by a local vendor with service support in Lagos, so
maintenance is never a problem.

Right now, we are running at a fraction of capacity because we need working
capital for raw materials. The machine is installed, tested, and ready to
run 2 shifts daily — we just need the inventory to feed it."

================================================================================
SCENE 3: OTHER EQUIPMENT (25 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Walk past the milling machines, mixing tanks, and other equipment.
Quick pan across the factory floor.

SCRIPT:
"Beyond the VFFS, we have milling machines for our pepper and date powder,
powder-holding tanks, and industrial mixers for our custard and chocolate
products. All equipment is in good condition and has been test-run. Our
technical manager, Kunle, ensures everything is maintained and ready."

================================================================================
SCENE 4: PRODUCT DISPLAY (30 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Pick up actual product sachets/packs. Show the DanFam brand.
Display a few different products — sugar sachets, pepper, chocolate powder.

SCRIPT:
"Here are the products. DanFam Granulated Sugar — 15g sachets, 500g and 1kg
packs, vitamin A fortified, NAFDAC approved. This is the first packaged
granulated sugar of its kind in Nigeria. We also have DanFam Chilli Pepper at
16% margin, and several other products: custard, chocolate powder, date powder,
and cereal.

The branding is clean, the packaging is professional, and consumers can
trust what they're buying. That's our competitive advantage."

================================================================================
SCENE 4B: FLOURISHSCENT — PERFUME LINE (25 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Show the FlourishScent area — perfume bottles, spray samples, bulk
drums/containers. Pick up a retail bottle and a sample vial. If available,
show the mixing/blending and filling area.

SCRIPT:
"We also run FlourishScent — our fragrance subsidiary. Perfume oils and sprays,
from 3ml sample vials up to 25kg bulk drums. We sell retail to consumers and
bulk to wholesalers and resellers across Lagos. Fragrance carries a much higher
margin than food, so this line adds profitability to the group's volume."

================================================================================
SCENE 5: DISTRIBUTION / STORAGE AREA (20 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Show raw material storage area and finished goods area. If there
is inventory, show it. If empty, explain why.

SCRIPT:
"This is where raw materials come in and finished goods go out. We already
have distributors in Lagos, Port Harcourt, and Aba — and an export channel
into Cameroon. When we have stock, it moves. The demand is confirmed."

================================================================================
SCENE 6: CLOSE — THE ASK (35 seconds)
================================================================================

VISUAL: Stand in the factory, look at the camera directly.

SCRIPT:
"Here's the honest truth: we have built the factory, installed the
equipment, secured NAFDAC approval for our food products, hired the team, and
built distributor relationships across three states — including an export
channel. FlourishScent is already selling perfume oils and sprays in Lagos,
retail and bulk. We are already generating revenue today even at skeletal
operations.

The only thing holding us back is working capital for raw materials.

With N50 million — N35 million for the food line and N15 million for the
perfume line — we can run at full capacity and unlock over N257 million in
combined monthly revenue and nearly N23 million in net monthly profit. The
equipment is ready. The market is ready. We just need capital to unlock it.

I invite you to review our numbers and schedule a visit. Thank you."

================================================================================
                      END OF SCRIPT
================================================================================

RECORDING TIPS:
1. Use your phone in LANDSCAPE mode (hold sideways)
2. Best time: 10am-2pm for natural light
3. Speak slowly and clearly — pause between points
4. If the machine is not running, still stand next to it — show it exists
5. One continuous take is fine — don't over-edit
6. Upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Google Drive and share the link
7. Embed the link in the pitch deck Slide 5 (next to "Equipment installed")

If the perfume production area is small or not visually rich, keep the
FlourishScent segment short — a few bottles and bulk drums on camera is enough.
The video should feel like WhatsApp voice note length — quick, real, honest.
