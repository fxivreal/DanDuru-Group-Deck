2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (One Page / ~200 Words)
================================================================================

DanDuru Group is a Lagos-based consumer products group with two subsidiaries:
DanDuru Industries (food manufacturing, founded October 2024) and FlourishScent
(perfumery, already selling). DanDuru has installed equipment, 13 food product
formulations, NAFDAC compliance, and distributor relationships in 3 states
(Lagos, Port Harcourt, Aba) — but currently operates skeletally due to lack of
working capital. It addresses a critical gap in bulk breaking: Nigeria imports
over 96% of its 1.6M-ton annual sugar consumption as bulk, and retail-pack
sugar imports are banned — so every sachet and pack sold to households must be
packaged in Nigeria. Yet the household segment (<20% of consumption, ~N50-60B estimate)
is served almost entirely by unhygienic informal "mallam" packers, with no
NAFDAC-approved, vitamin-fortified packaged granulated sugar for the mass
market. DanDuru buys that bulk from the refiners and repacks it into fortified
15g sachets, 500g and 1kg home packs.

FlourishScent sources scarce undiluted perfume oil from importers and blends,
fills and packages it — retail packs from 3ml and bulk fragrance oils up to
25kg for wholesalers and resellers — serving a Nigerian fragrance market that
is overwhelmingly import-dependent. As a higher-margin
line (blended 38.8% gross across all SKUs vs ~9% blended for food),
it diversifies group earnings and
shares the group's distribution, finance and regulatory infrastructure.

At full capacity, the group is projected to generate N282.5M monthly revenue
and N33.9M net monthly profit — an annual run rate of N407.2M. FlourishScent
scales from N2.5M/month today to N45M/month over a 6-month post-deployment
ramp; even at a conservative 30% blended margin the group still clears N360.0M
annually. The Nigerian
food-beverage market exceeds N240B
in sugar alone, with cocoa beverages adding N90B+, and DanDuru targets 10-20%
share per segment by 2030.

DanDuru Group is raising N50,000,000 as working capital (debt or equity — open
to discussion): N35M for food raw materials and capacity, N15M to scale the
perfume line. The facility seeds the first production cycle, then revolves as
revenue is recycled into inventory. Capital is the only missing piece —
products, equipment, people,
and routes to market are already in place.
