Competition & Differentiation

DanDuru Group occupies the sweet spot: high compliance + low price

DanDuru Group ★ (Sweet Spot)

High quality/compliance. Low price. NAFDAC-approved, vitamin-fortified food + fragrance sourced direct from importers at mass-market price.

St. Louis / Golden Penny

Premium pricing, table sugar only. Not focused on mass-market packaged granulated sugar. Limited SKU range.

Informal "Mallam" Packers

Low price but no NAFDAC, no fortification, no hygiene, no brand. Regulatory moat protects DanDuru.

Imported Fragrance

Undiluted perfume oil is scarce locally. FlourishScent buys directly from importers and resells it at accessible prices — from 3ml samples up to 25kg drums.

Dangote, Bua & Golden Sugar sell 50kg bulk to factories — they're our feedstock, not our rivals.

1. Regulatory Moat

Government mandates vitamin A fortification — informal packers cannot comply without capital. DanDuru is already compliant.

2. Cost Advantage

VFFS automation packs sugar at a lower per-unit cost than hand-packing — quality control, pack flexibility and restock speed.

3. Brand Trust

DanFam = hygiene, quality, traceability. Institutional buyers can only buy NAFDAC-approved products.

Monthly service ≈N1.9M (principal + interest, ~20%/yr) is covered 10x+ every month of the ramp (slide 12). Agreed term: 36 months, amortized monthly.
